HHF-ADCTF

In an AD CTF, every team owns a vulnerable service stack. The goal is to harden your own boxes while stealing flags from everyone else. A central control plane tracks service health (SLA), distributes fresh flags, and scores valid captures in near real-time.

Use this portal to register teams, rotate flags, review the attack log, and follow the scoreboard as the tug-of-war unfolds.
